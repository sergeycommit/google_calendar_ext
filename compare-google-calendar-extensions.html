User-first comparison

Choose the Google Calendar extension that matches your working style.

Three Chrome extensions cover most “see my Google Calendar without opening a tab” needs: Checker Plus for Google Calendar™ (300,000 users, 4.4★) for maximum features, Button for Google Calendar (100,000 users, 4.1★) for a light popup with one-click joins, and Calendar Extension for Google Calendar™ (7,000 users, 4.5★, free) for a day timeline with time-to-event chips. The best fit depends on how you check your schedule during the day, not on the longest feature list. Chrome Web Store data checked September 18, 2026.

Three toolbar extensions, three styles

Calendar Extension for Google Calendar™ (ours)

Day timeline, time-to-event chips, quick add, and one-click Meet, Zoom, and Teams joins. 7,000 users, 4.5★ from 44 ratings, free.

Feature-dense: Checker Plus

Checker Plus for Google Calendar™ by Jason Savard shows upcoming events, meeting notifications, and snoozing without opening Google Calendar. 300,000 users, 4.4★ from 2.1K ratings; offers in-app purchases.

Lightweight: Button for Google Calendar

Button for Google Calendar by BrowseCraft checks your schedule and joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls in one click. 100,000 users, 4.1★ from 209 ratings, free.
